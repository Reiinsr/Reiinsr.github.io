AI Strategy Deck Generator

Compresses ~a month of strategist & designer work into 20–30 minutes.

▼ View Sample Output Deck

Problem · Building a strategic communications proposal deck for a Dubai marketing & PR consultancy took a team of strategists and designers close to a month per client.

Build · A two-phase n8n pipeline triggered by a single email brief. Phase 1 (Research): a JavaScript node parses the brief and builds a research prompt sent to Anthropic Claude with web search, returning real, sourced market, competitor, and audience research as structured JSON. Phase 2 (Design): a second prompt drives Python (python-pptx) to design and build a polished, on-brand .pptx deck — section dividers, stakeholder maps, media mapping, and dynamic accent colors pulled from the research.

Engineering · Splitting the job into two separate LLM calls with an async batch submission and a poll/wait loop cut runtime from ~45 min to 20–30 min — and deliberately worked around the platform's 10-minute execution cap on the starter tier, avoiding a costly plan upgrade for long-running jobs.

Result · End-to-end deck generation with no manual research or design, delivered straight to the strategy team's inbox.
